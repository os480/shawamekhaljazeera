MHSF Fire Safety Cabinet Al-Sabeh is a sturdy and dependable enclosure designed to store fire safety equipment safely. Made from premium materials such as stainless steel or galvanized steel with a thickness of 1mm to 2mm, this cabinet ensures durability and protection in various conditions.


The cabinet has a corrosion-resistant coating, making it suitable for outdoor environments. Its temperature tolerance ranges from -40°C to +60°C, ensuring the contents stay secure even in harsh weather conditions.


It features a high-quality door seal that prevents water ingress, safeguarding the internal fire equipment.


The cabinet models are MHSF-07 for the red model, and MHSF-07-S for the stainless steel model. Both models have dimensions of Width: 710mm, Height: 780mm, Depth: 210mm, offering a compact yet spacious design to accommodate essential fire safety gear.


The paint finish is a durable, matte epoxy in red, providing high resistance to environmental factors and maintaining the cabinet’s appearance over time. Furthermore, each cabinet includes clear usage instructions in both Arabic and English.
