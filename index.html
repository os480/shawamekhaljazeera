FM-200 fire suppression

Clean-agent suppression for critical spaces

A clean fire-suppressing agent that protects sensitive electronic equipment without leaving water or residue. Safe for occupied spaces and used in hospitals, data centers, and more.
02

Fire alarm systems

Early detection that saves lives

A network of devices that work together to detect fire and warn people. Sensors as eyes and ears, a control panel as the brain, and alarms that signal the building to evacuate.
03

Fire sprinkler systems

Instant automatic suppression

A network of pipes with sprinkler heads that release water when temperature rises above a set point. A giant shower activated by heat, not a button.
04

Fire extinguishers

The first line of defense
