The AWWA C515 Resilient Seated OS&Y Gate Valve with flange ends (Model No. Z41X-03) is designed for reliable performance and durability in waterworks and industrial applications. This valve features a robust construction with ductile iron and EPDM components, ensuring long-lasting service even under high pressure and harsh conditions.


Product Information:


The valve is available in various sizes and working pressures, adhering to the AWWA C515 valve standard and ASME B16.1 Class 125 flange standard. It operates effectively within a temperature range of 0°C to 80°C, making it suitable for a wide range of environments and applications.


Key features include:


  • Working pressure rating of 300 PSI.
  • Durable construction with a ductile iron body and bonnet.
  • EPDM gaskets and sealing rings for reliable sealing.
  • Stainless steel or bronze stems for added strength and corrosion resistance.
  • Bronze sliding disc for smooth operation.
  • Carbon steel screws with hot galvanization for enhanced durability.
  • Ductile iron handwheel for ease of use.
