The System Sensor 2WT-B Conventional Photoelectric Smoke and Heat Detector, is designed for reliable smoke and heat detection with ease of installation and maintenance. This detector combines advanced smoke and thermal sensing technology to ensure accurate and timely detection of fire hazards, making it an ideal choice for safety and security applications.


Key features include:
  • 85 dB sounder for audible alerts.
  • Form C relay for versatile connectivity.
  • Isolated thermal sensor for accurate heat detection (135°F / 57°C).
  • Plug-in design with included base for easy installation.
  • In-line terminals for secure connections.
  • Flexible mounting options for various installation needs.
  • Stop-Drop 'N Lock™ attachment to the base for quick and secure mounting
  • Removable cover and chamber for easy maintenance.
  • Remote maintenance signalling for convenience.
  • Drift compensation and smoothing algorithms to reduce nuisance alarms.
  • Simplified sensitivity measurement for accurate monitoring.
  • Dual-colour LEDs for clear status indication.

Product Specifications:
  • Model: 2WT-B
  • Type: Conventional Photoelectric Smoke and Heat Detector
  • Operating Voltage: 12/24V non-polarized
  • Alarm Current: 130 mA max, limited by the control panel
  • Sound Output: 85 dB
  • Operating Temperature Range: 32°F to 100°F (0°C to 37.8°C)
  • Operating Humidity Range: 0 to 95% RH non-condensing
  • Dimensions: 5.3 inches (134 mm) diameter, 2.0 inches (51 mm) height
  • Weight: 7.1 oz (200 g)
  • Mounting: 3½-inch octagonal back box, 4-inch octagonal back box, single-gang back box, 4-inch square back box with a plaster ring, or direct mount to ceiling

Additional Features:
  • Installation ease with installer-friendly base and plug-in design.
  • Intelligent design to reduce nuisance alarms with drift compensation and smoothing algorithms.
  • Instant inspection with red and green LEDs for status indication.
