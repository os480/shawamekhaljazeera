Fire Sprinkler System

What is a Fire Sprinkler System?

  • A network of pipes equipped with sprinkler heads that spray water when heat rises

    above a certain temperature. Think of it like a giant shower system activated by heat

    instead of a button.

  • It's installed in homes, buildings, and businesses to help control fires and keep

    people safe. Like having a built-in firefighter ready to respond instantly.

  • It comes in different types, but all work by spraying water to cool down the fire and

    prevent it from spreading, giving you multiple lines of defense against a fire.

Why a Fire Sprinkler System?


Benefits

  • They react to fires in seconds, preventing the fire from spreading.

  • By quickly controlling fires, sprinklers can help prevent injuries and save lives.

  • Sprinklers can also minimize damage to your building.

Types of Fire
Sprinkler Systems

  • Wet pipe: Most common, suitable for standard buildings.

  • Dry pipe: Used in unheated buildings or where freezing

    temperatures are a concern.

  • Deluge: Opens all sprinkler heads at once, ideal for rapid

    fire spread areas.

  • Pre-action: Combines features of wet and dry pipe, good

    for areas sensitive to water damage.
