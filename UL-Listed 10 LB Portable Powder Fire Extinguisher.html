The Buckeye UL-Listed 10HI SA80 ABC Fire Extinguisher is designed to deliver reliable, versatile, and robust fire protection.   • Model: 10HI SA80 ABC
  • Agent Capacity: 10 lb (4.54 kg), ABC Dry Chemical
  • Fire Rating: UL-rated 4-A:80-B:C, suitable for Class A (ordinary combustibles), Class B (flammable liquids), and Class C (electrical equipment) fires
