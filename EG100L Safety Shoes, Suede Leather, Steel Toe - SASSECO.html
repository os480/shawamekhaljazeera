The EG100L Safety Shoes, Suede Leather, Steel Toe deliver a robust combination of workplace protection, durability, and all-day comfort. Designed for demanding industrial and construction environments, the EG100L Safety Shoes, Suede Leather, Steel Toe feature a premium suede leather upper paired with a breathable mesh lining to keep feet comfortable during long shifts. Equipped with a heavy-duty steel toe cap and a specialized moulded EVA outsole, the EG100L Safety Shoes, Suede Leather, Steel Toe are engineered to guard against impacts, slips, and hazardous liquids while providing excellent heel energy absorption to minimize fatigue.


Key Features


  • Safety Toecap:Integrated steel toe provides superior protection against heavy impacts and compression.
  • Slip-Resistant Outsole:Engineered to maintain firm traction on slick or uneven surfaces, reducing the risk of workplace falls.
  • Oil and Fuel Resistant:The rugged outsole is specially treated to withstand degradation from industrial oils and fuels.
  • Heel Energy Absorption:Built-in shock absorption in the heel area reduces impact on joints and minimizes foot and leg fatigue.
  • Water Resistance:Designed to resist water penetration and absorption, keeping feet dry in damp conditions.
  • Lightweight Flexibility:Constructed without a steel or Kevlar midsole plate to offer a lighter weight and more flexible fit for active workers.

Technical Specifications


  • Upper Material:Suede Leather
  • Toe Protection:Steel Toe
  • Midsole:No Steel or Kevlar Plate
  • Outsole Material:Moulded EVA
  • Inner Lining:Breathable Mesh
  • Size Range (EU):36 - 46 (Also accommodates corresponding UK/AU 3.5-11.0, US 4.0-12.0, and KOR 235-300 sizing)

Safety Standards & Certifications


The EG100L Safety Shoes, Suede Leather, Steel Toe are rigorously tested and certified to meet leading international safety footwear standards, ensuring reliable performance on the job site:


  • Safety Standard Classification:SB PS WPA SR E FO
  • ASTM Standard:ASTM F2413:2018
  • EN ISO Standard:EN ISO 20345:2022
