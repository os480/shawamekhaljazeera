FM-200 Fire Suppression System

What is FM-200?

  • A clean agent fire suppressant that protects sensitive electronics without leaving water or residue.

  • Safe for occupied spaces, used in hospitals, data centers, and more.

  • Deploys quickly (within 10 seconds) and extinguishes fires effectively.

Why choose FM-200?


Benefits

  • Ideal for protecting mission-critical equipment like computers and telecommunications systems.

  • Takes up less space than other fire suppression options.

  • Safe for people and the environment.

SASSECO Offers

  • Regular inspections and maintenance to ensure your system is compliant with NFPA and Civil Defense standards.

  • 24/7 response to fire suppression system discharges.

  • FM-200 recharge services to keep your system operational.

How it works

  • FM-200 uses a special gas that absorbs heat from burning materials, stopping the fire.

  • It's stored in cylinders and released through pipes when a fire is detected.
