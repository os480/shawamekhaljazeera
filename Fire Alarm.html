Fire Alarm Systems

What is a Fire Alarm System?

  • A fire alarm system is a network of devices that work together to detect and warn
    people about a fire. It's like a team of firefighters on the lookout for danger!

  • Sensors: These are like the firefighters' eyes and ears. They can be smoke detectors,
    heat detectors, or even carbon monoxide detectors. When they sense something suspicious, they send a signal.

  • Control panel: This is the brain of the system. It receives the signal from the sensors
    and decides what to do next.

  • Alarms: These are the loudspeakers or flashing lights that let everyone know there's
    a fire. They're like the firefighters' sirens, urging everyone to evacuate.

General Overview of Fire Alarm Systems


Benefits
