Bristol Fire Pump Systems are fully customizable and made-to-order!

Bristol Fire Pump Systems represent a comprehensive suite of firefighting solutions designed to meet diverse fire protection needs across industrial, commercial, and residential applications. The system offers exceptional flexibility through its multiple pump configurations and customization options.


Core Pump Types:

  • Horizontal Split Case Pumps: Featuring a double suction design with superior efficiency and low maintenance requirements, ideal for high-capacity applications up to 3000 GPM
  • End Suction Pumps: and versatile units perfect for spaces where installation footprint is a concern, available in both electric and engine-driven configurations
  • Vertical Turbine Pumps: Specialized design for underground water sources and applications where the water level is below ground level, ensuring constant prime maintenance

Delivery Options:

Containerized
Systems		 Complete plug-and-play solutions housed in
weather-proof containers (20ft or 40ft options)
Packaged
Systems		 Skid-mounted configurations combining pumps,
controllers, and accessories in a single unit
Custom Solutions Tailored systems designed to specific project
requirements and space constraints


Key Features:

  • Compliance with NFPA requirements and international standards
  • UL/FM listed options available for certified applications
  • Integrated control systems with automatic operation capability
  • Comprehensive range of auxiliary components including fuel tanks and anti-vortex plates
  • Factory-tested assemblies ensure reliable performance
  • Advanced engineering support for custom applications

System Integration: Bristol's fire pump systems can be seamlessly integrated with:


  • Building management systems
  • Emergency power supplies
  • Advanced monitoring and control interfaces
  • Existing fire protection infrastructure

Each system is backed by Bristol's extensive engineering expertise and global support network, ensuring reliable fire protection solutions for any application scenario.
