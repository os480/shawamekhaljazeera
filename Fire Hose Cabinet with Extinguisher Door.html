Manufactured from durable steel with a thickness of 1mm to 2mm, this cabinet offers robustness and resilience for long-lasting protection.


The MHS-02 cabinet features rounded, folded edges for safe handling and is coated with a red polyester resin texture finish, providing enhanced resistance to wear and environmental factors. With additional rust protection from a zinc phosphate pretreatment, the cabinet is designed to endure challenging conditions without compromising on aesthetics.


The cabinet measures Width: 1050mm, Height: 800mm, Depth: 230mm, offering ample storage space for essential fire equipment. It includes two knockout holes for left or right water entry, ensuring flexible installation options. The cabinet also includes a double door system with a 180-degree opening for easy access.


Key features include provisions for a 10mm diameter fixing, adjustable nozzle, 1.5" brass fittings for hose connection, and compatibility with a 1x30 meter high-tensile synthetic rubber fire hose. The cabinet comes in red. Instructions are available in both English and Arabic.


Note that it does not come with a fire extinguisher included, it has a fire extinguisher door.


Note: Stainless Steel, Recessed Type: Only the door and frame are stainless steel. The surface mount is fully stainlesssteel.
